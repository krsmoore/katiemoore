How I Work

Start with
the problem,
not the solution.

I ask a lot of questions. Five Whys is a good start. I’m more of a Fifty Whys person.

I want to understand what’s actually happening, what people actually need, and why we’re doing things the way we are. People rarely build systems intending for them to fail. So before I declare something broken, I want to know what problem it was built to solve — and whether that problem still exists.

Then I figure out what’s worth keeping, what’s getting in the way, and what would work better — and build from there.
