01 / See it differently

The problem wasn’t the programs.
It was the story connecting them.

The pattern kept showing up: introduce Walmart Fulfillment Services, then say, “If that’s not the right solution for you, look at this.” The unintended message was that everything else was a consolation prize.

Program. Pitch. Next program. We were asking people to understand the portfolio one offering at a time.

03 / The north star
“Does this bring us closer to the goal or farther from it?”

Whenever the work started drifting toward old habits, I didn’t debate the individual change. I asked this question. It gave a large cross-functional team a shared decision rule without requiring me to make every decision.

05 / Look back

The thinking behind the work.

“How it comes to life is their discretion.
What comes to life is not.”
What I’m proud of

We moved incredibly fast without turning it into “Katie’s project.” We brought people along, challenged familiar ways of thinking, and crossed the finish line together.

My guiding principle

Alignment doesn’t require agreement on every decision. It requires agreement on how decisions get made.

If I had a do-over

I wouldn’t manufacture one. I’m genuinely happy with how we approached this project.
