The mapped experience

One seller. Eight different moments.

What I did

I listened to calls, spoke with sellers, and reviewed available data. I identified patterns of similar activity across seller behavior. From there, I established language to describe the experiences, what they included, and the common pain points.

The execution

One north star.
Many contributors.

Our shared decision rule
✧
Does this bring us closer to the goal or farther from it?

A shared test for every change, without requiring me to make every decision.

I built the foundation

I created the lifecycle framework and wrote the core copy for associate learning and seller-facing materials.

We brought it to life

Instructional designers, product and program SMEs, brand designers, Marketing, Legal and copy reviewers helped bring it to life.

How it comes to life is their discretion.
What comes to life is not.
The reflection

The work became
our project.

What I’m proud of

We moved incredibly fast without turning it into “Katie’s project.” We brought people along, challenged familiar ways of thinking, and crossed the finish line together.

My guiding principle

Alignment doesn’t require agreement on every decision. It requires agreement on how decisions get made.

If I had a do-over

I wouldn’t manufacture one. I’m genuinely happy with how we approached this project.

A clearer story. A shared decision rule. One language across the experience.
