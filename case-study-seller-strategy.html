Katie MooreWALMART / 2025–2026
The situation

Twenty programs. One missing story.

What was happening

Our sales process sounded like a checklist of programs. Prospective sellers struggled to understand how the offerings fit their needs, and sales associates struggled to explain them. That confusion made it easier for sellers to decline our support and look elsewhere.

Tenured associates dutifully introduced as the premier fulfillment option, unintentionally making everything else sound like a consolation prize. Different parts of the organization had been established at different times, and our pitch reflected that history. We had a portfolio of capabilities, but no shared story.

My starting point

I needed a way to position our two fulfillment suites as complementary options, helping sellers find the right fit within our portfolio instead of turning to a competitor. The framework had to be easy for associates to learn, explain, and use across the organization.

We had plenty of programs. What we didn’t have was a way to talk about them as one solution.
90 days to launch13+ partner groups
The decision

I stopped thinking
about programs.

Each program lead treated their own program as the top priority. Every update or improvement brought another request for support, but there was no shared framework for deciding what mattered most across the portfolio.

The result was conflicting priorities, redundant work, and overloaded teams. Effort didn’t consistently translate into value, and internal messaging reflected competing program agendas rather than a coherent story.

Program. Pitch. Next program.

Each offering came with its own story and its own claim to priority. We were asking people to understand the portfolio one program at a time—and leaving them to figure out how the pieces fit together.

The real problem: sellers never saw the entire portfolio. They had challenges they didn't know we could solve.

Walmart Fulfillment Services

When selling on Walmart.com, sellers can choose three ways to ship their products to the customers who ordered them: they can ship it themselves (Seller Fulfillment Solutions), they can choose a company to handle it (3P Solutions), or they can let Walmart handle it (Walmart Fulfillment Services, or WFS).

WFS lets sellers send their inventory into the fulfillment center, where it's safely and securely stored. When a customer places an order on Walmart.com, WFS picks the item, packs the box, and ships the order out to the customer, and also handles returns and customer services.
